THE CHALLENGE

What makes Construction recruitment hard?

01

Speed decides the placement

On contract and permanent roles, the first agency with a credible candidate often gets the opportunity.

02

Tickets and certifications matter

Safety cards, licences and qualifications need to be considered before a candidate is presented.

03

Commercial roles are scarce

Experienced estimators and quantity surveyors can be difficult to find and require targeted outreach.
